How this works

1. Read the PDF. pdf.js opens each transcript in your browser and pulls out the text and the x/y position of every character on the page — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

2. Rebuild the rows. Characters are grouped back into subject rows (code, name, credits, marks, grade) using their column positions on the page.

3. Compute with exact fractions. A small Rat (rational-number) class stores every value as an exact fraction — never a JavaScript float — so SGPA, CGPA and percentages can't drift from rounding.

4. Recompute, don't trust. SGPA is recalculated from subject rows; CGPA is checked using whichever formula is selected in Step 2 — Old: Σ(semester credits × printed SGPA) ÷ Total Credits, or New: Σ(course credit × grade point from marks) ÷ Total Credits, across every course. Header identity fields and totals are checked independently.

5. Compare. Every recomputed figure is checked against what's printed on the transcript; mismatches are flagged in the results table below.

6. Self-test on load. A short built-in test (see the console) checks the arithmetic engine against known cases every time the page runs, so a bug would show up immediately rather than silently producing a wrong grade.

Rules in force

Credit structure T-P-PR summed (0-4-0 → 4, never 0)  ·  Per-semester credit sum must equal the semester's printed CREDITS line — checked first; a mismatch is flagged before the results are trusted. This doubles as the parse-integrity check: if the two-column reader had mis-assigned a row, the credits would not reconcile and the discrepancy is surfaced rather than silently accepted.  ·  SGPA = Σ(Ci × Gi) ÷ ΣCi, 1 decimal, ROUND_HALF_UP  ·  CGPA — formula selected in Step 2: Old = Σ(semester credits × printed SGPA) ÷ printed Total Credits (every decimal used exactly as printed), or New = Σ(course credit × grade point from marks) ÷ Total Credits, summed across every course, with the grade point taken from that course's marks, not its printed letter; either way the final figure gets Excel ROUND(CGPA, 1)  ·  Year of Passing must be 2025; Programme and Specialization are checked against the expected text entered above  ·  Grade re-derived from marks and checked against the printed letter.

Derived totals: Total Credits = Σ(T+P+PR), Obtained Marks = Σ subject marks (with per-semester subtotals), Maximum Marks = unique course codes × 100 (each code like FDP101, AAL108 counts as one subject worth 100 marks; the printed MAX. MARKS is cross-checked against this count — e.g. 35 codes × 100 = 3500 must match the printed 3500), Percentage = Obtained ÷ printed Maximum × 100, then Excel ROUND(Percentage, 1). A Maximum Marks anomaly is reported, never silently substituted.

Arithmetic: every value is an exact fraction of two BigIntegers. No IEEE-754 float and no tolerance window in the rounding path — a value either matches or it does not. Printed figures are compared at the precision they were printed.

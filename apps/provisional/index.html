1Upload TR per semester — Regular (left) & Improvement (right)
How it works: for each semester, upload the Regular TR on the left and its Improvement / Supplementary TR on the right (only if it had one). A TR file can hold multiple sheets — after uploading, use the dropdown to pick the correct sheet.

Comparison rule: for every subject, the higher of Regular vs Improvement total is kept. Example: Reg 15 & Imp 30 → 30   Reg 16 & Imp 12 → 16   equal → either. A student passes overall only if no subject in any semester is F after this update.

Withheld rule: a W in the Grace column withholds the result — one W in one subject of one semester is enough, in the regular or the improvement TR, and it applies even when that paper is declared P. Withheld students are excluded from the Final Result (Provisional) sheet and reported in the Withheld sheet instead.

The downloadable workbook contains 8 sheets: Regular, Supplementary, Updated Marks, Final Result (Provisional), Withheld, Fail — No Provisional, plus Extracted Regular TR and Extracted Supplementary TR holding every value the tool read, with source sheet and row number, for manual checking.

Withheld rule: if a W appears in a Grace cell — in any semester, in any subject, in the regular or the improvement TR, and even if that paper and every other paper is a pass — the student's result is withheld. Those students are left out of Final Result (Provisional) and out of Fail — No Provisional, and are listed instead in the Withheld sheet with enrolment, name, marks and the exact subject(s) carrying the W.
